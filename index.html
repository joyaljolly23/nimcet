What is NIMCET?

NIMCET (NIT MCA Common Entrance Test) is a national-level entrance exam conducted by NITs on rotation. It is the gateway for MCA admission at 11 NITs across India.

Exam Pattern

120 MCQs · 120 minutes · +4 correct, –1 wrong. Subjects: Maths (50%), CS (20%), Reasoning (15%), English (10%), GK (5%). Held annually in June.

Eligibility

Any graduate with Maths at 10+2 or graduation level. Minimum 60% marks (55% for SC/ST). No upper age limit. Final-year students can also apply.
