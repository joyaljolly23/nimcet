What is NIMCET?

NIMCET (NIT MCA Common Entrance Test) is a national-level entrance exam conducted by one of the NITs on a rotational basis. It is the gateway for admission to the MCA (Master of Computer Applications) programme at 11 NITs across India.

Exam Pattern

120 MCQ questions · 120 minutes · +4 for correct, –1 for wrong. Subjects: Mathematics (50%), CS (20%), Reasoning (15%), English (10%), GK (5%). Conducted once a year, usually in June.

Eligibility

Any graduate with Mathematics at 10+2 level or at graduation. Minimum 60% marks in graduation (55% for SC/ST). Age limit: No upper age limit. Students in their final year of graduation can also apply.
